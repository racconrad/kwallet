# Bienvenida

Mode: Operate, with a gentle introduction to the companion.

Confirmed brief: warm, quiet adoption; the pet is yours; short Spanish copy, real sprites, shapes and Material icons; no emoji or technical explanations.

Direction: extend the existing warm cream/forest Material theme. A large baby sprite arrives into a soft arched stage, then remains the protagonist of selection, naming, a first greeting, and the final welcome home. Five short moments with a fixed bottom action and Back. One main action per moment. No feature-card wall, XP explanation, infrastructure explanation or compulsory permission prompt.

First viewport: the actual animated baby companion, a simple arch/ground, the headline “Hay alguien esperándote.” and “Quiero conocerle”. A returning user can bring their data.

Signature interaction: pet approaches at first meeting; a first touch on the naming screen produces a short heart-icon response and a celebration. The final screen shows the chosen pet and name as the user's companion. Respect Remove animations.

Keep: currency setup including user-defined symbols and optional extra currencies, notification opt-in, recovery/import, returning-user bypass. Currency and reminders are compact controls rather than instructional pages. Existing pixel atlases are the visual source, not newly generated illustrations.

Build: code-first native Flutter. Review on phone/tablet and light/dark through Flutter raster captures; no APK or emulator install in this task due to the existing user constraint. Do not claim hardware verification.
